Skin disease classifier

An image classifier for 23 skin-condition categories, trained on the DermNet dataset by fine-tuning an ImageNet ResNet-18.

  1. A lens scans each photo, the way the model sees it at 224 × 224.
  2. Each photo gets a ranked guess across 23 categories.
  3. Under the hood: ResNet-18 features into a new 23-way head.
  4. Trained in two phases: head only (lr 1e-3), then all layers (lr 1e-4, cosine).

Brass Rail Poker

Texas Hold'em that teaches you while you play. Coach Mae reads your hand, counts outs, runs a Monte Carlo estimate and tells you what to do.

  1. The dealer shuffles and deals. Mae rates your starting hand.
  2. On the flop she counts your outs with the rule of 2 and 4.
  3. She compares your chance of winning with the pot odds.
  4. She recommends fold, check, call or raise, with a reason.

AI Study Assistant

Upload class materials and ask questions about them. Answers cite the passages they came from, and the app turns your notes into flashcards and quizzes.

  1. PDFs, DOCX and notes are split into chunks.
  2. Each chunk is embedded and stored in ChromaDB.
  3. Your question is embedded too, and the nearest chunks are retrieved.
  4. The model answers from those chunks and cites them.
  5. The same chunks become flashcards and practice quizzes.

#MD-05/05C++17 · ARM NEON

C++ inference engine

A neural network engine written from scratch, built on a matrix multiply I tuned by hand in five measured stages. No ML frameworks.

  1. Naive i-j-k loop. It walks down columns of B, so every load misses cache.
  2. Reorder to i-k-j: rows stream and clang auto-vectorizes. About 18× faster.
  3. Cache blocking keeps tiles hot.
  4. A hand-written NEON micro-kernel does 4-wide fused multiply-adds.
  5. Spread across 15 cores with dynamic scheduling over P and E cores.
  6. Checked against Apple Accelerate on every run, then used to train an MNIST MLP.
